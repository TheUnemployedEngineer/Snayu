A few weeks ago I realized something a little embarrassing.

I'd used neural networks plenty of times, but I couldn't really explain what was happening inside one. I just called model.fit() and hoped for the best.

So I decided to build one from scratch. No PyTorch, no TensorFlow, just NumPy and a lot of trial and error.

I called it Snayu (স্নায়ু). It's Bengali for "nerve."

I wrote every forward pass, every gradient and every weight update by hand. Honestly, the first time XOR finally trained correctly, I stared at the screen like I'd discovered fire.

The part I'm proudest of is the last demo. I trained a small network to learn a bumpy 3D surface, and recorded it as it learned. It begins as a shapeless blob and slowly turns into a near-perfect copy of the target. It's so satisfying to watch (GIF below 👇).
